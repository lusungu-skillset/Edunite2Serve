Edunite2serve and Give HOPE: Supporting Wayfinders Students Club
At Edunite2serve Malawi, we are thrilled to work hand in hand with Give HOPE to support the Wayfinders Students Club from Malawi Adventist University. This collaboration focuses on empowering students to engage in meaningful community outreach initiatives that make a positive impact.
Our Mission:
Together, we aim to:
Empower Students:
Equip Wayfinders members with the skills and resources needed to lead community outreach programs.
Address Local Needs:
Tackle pressing social issues through volunteer efforts, spreading hope and support in the communities we serve.
Promote Leadership and Service:
Foster a spirit of service and leadership among students, encouraging them to contribute positively to society.
Key Messages:
Empower Through Service
Creating Positive Change
Nurturing Future Leaders
Together We Can Make a Difference
Compassion in Action
Every Contribution Counts
