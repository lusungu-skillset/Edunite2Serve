Edunite2serve and Becky: Vegetarian Cookery Training in Zomba
Edunite2serve Malawi, in collaboration with Becky, recently hosted a successful Vegetarian Cookery Training in Zomba. This initiative aimed to enhance culinary skills and promote healthy eating habits among participants.
Highlights of the Training:
Skill Development:
Trainees learned a variety of vegetarian recipes, focusing on nutrition and the benefits of a plant-based diet.
Empowerment through Cooking:
Participants gained confidence in their cooking abilities, encouraging them to prepare healthy meals for themselves and their families.
Community Engagement:
The training fostered a sense of community as participants shared experiences and knowledge, promoting a culture of healthy living.
Key Messages:
Empowering Through Knowledge
Healthy Eating for a Healthier Community
Cultivating Skills for Lifelong Benefits
Together We Can Promote Wellness
Compassion in Action
