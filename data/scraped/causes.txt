Lake Side Edunite2serve Farm
The Lakeside Edunite2serve Farming initiative aims to cultivate various types of crops to support underprivileged students at Malawi Adventist University, as well as the surrounding communities. This project not only provides essential resources for students in need but also fosters sustainable agricultural practices that benefit the local population. Through this initiative, we strive to create opportunities for growth and development, ensuring a brighter future for both students and community members alike.

Mangunda Adventist Primary School
Mangunda Seventh Day Primary School, located in Thyolo, is a vital educational institution serving 200 plus learners in classes 1 through 7. Despite its importance to the community, the school faces significant challenges due to inadequate classroom facilities, forcing some students to face hard time during their learning time.
