Our Story
Edunite2Serve was founded by Professor Sharon Pittman. Seeing a need for energetic, nonprofit mission work in Africa, Edunite2Serve was formed to provide sensible Christ-centered sustainable solutions. We are exploring new projects and need a world of amazing partners to join us.
Edunite2Serve partners with its USA counterpart in Atlanta, Georgia who serves as a 501c3 to receive donations
The Goal
Our Mission
Edunite2Serve is a fully volunteer organization that is dedicated to enhancing whole living to those challenged by poverty in south-central Africa.
The Outlook
Our Vission
Edunite2Serve exists to empower communities through education, humanitarian service, and sustainable development.
We are committed to building resilience and fostering hope by uniting evidence-based research, compassionate outreach, and faith-driven service—transforming lives and strengthening local capacity for long-term impact.

Community Involvement
We believe in supporting our community and giving back through various initiatives. Whether it’s volunteering for local garden projects or sponsoring environmental education programs, we are dedicated to making a positive impact.

Frequently

What is EduNite2Serve?
EduNite2Serve is a non-profit organization focused on enhancing educational opportunities and promoting community service in underserved areas.
How can I get involved with EduNite2Serve?
You can get involved by volunteering, donating, or participating in our community events and educational programs. Check our website for current opportunities.
Can I donate to support your initiatives?
Yes, donations are welcome! You can donate through our website, where you can choose to support specific programs or general operations.
Are donations tax-deductible?
Yes, donations may be tax-deductible, depending on your country’s tax laws. Please consult your tax advisor for more details.
Can I organize an event to support EduNite2Serve?
Absolutely! We encourage community members to organize events to raise awareness and funds for our mission. Please contact us for guidance and support.
