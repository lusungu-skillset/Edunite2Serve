Give HOPE Partnership with Edunite2serve at Neno Montessori School
At Edunite2serve Malawi, we are excited to announce our partnership with Give HOPE in support of Neno Montessori School in the Neno District. This collaboration aims to uplift the lives of orphans and vulnerable children, ensuring they receive the education, care, and support they need to thrive.
Supporting Orphans at Mangunda Adventist Primary School, Thyolo
Our mission also extends to the orphans at Mangunda Adventist Primary School in Thyolo. We are dedicated to providing essential financial and material support to these children, ensuring they have access to education, healthcare, and basic necessities.
Both initiatives face numerous challenges, but through charity and donations, we can address these social issues and create nurturing environments for growth and development.
